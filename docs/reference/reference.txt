CYBERPUNK NEO-FUTURISTIC ART DIRECTION

Blend the premium ASUS ROG-style hardware showcase with a cyberpunk-inspired futuristic atmosphere.

The experience should feel like:

- a next-generation military technology reveal
- a futuristic megacorporation product launch
- an underground cyber-tech showroom
- a high-performance machine from the year 2077

Do NOT copy Cyberpunk 2077 assets, UI, logos, characters, or exact designs.

Instead, capture the broader visual language:

- neon-lit futuristic environments
- high-tech industrial design
- holographic interfaces
- digital augmentation aesthetics
- dense information displays
- cinematic sci-fi atmosphere
